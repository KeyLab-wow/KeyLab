# KeyLab

KeyLab brings your Mythic+ runs, raid nights, practice tests, gear plans, and macro sequences together in one personal performance journal.

***

## What You Can Do

*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Encounters</strong></span> — Review recorded Mythic+ runs and raid boss pulls
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Summary</strong></span> — See your latest Mythic+ run or raid night at a glance
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Talent Builds</strong></span> — Compare talent builds with your recorded results
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Stat Profiles</strong></span> — Compare stat priorities and see how they performed for you
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Gear Profiles</strong></span> — Compare complete gear setups with your recorded results
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Trends</strong></span> — Follow changes in your performance over time
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Practice</strong></span> — Record timed training-dummy sessions and compare your results
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Gear Planning</strong></span> — Learn the season’s main gearing choices and tools
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Gear Targets</strong></span> — Choose current-season gear or use the Stat Goal Matcher
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Stat Goal Matcher</strong></span> — Find gear combinations that move you toward your stat goals
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Gear Dashboard</strong></span> — Track Tier progress, gear targets, alternatives, and item sources
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Macro Sequencer</strong></span> — Build macro sequences with keyboard or mouse-button bindings
*   <span style="color:#ecf0f1"><strong>Settings</strong></span> — Export, import, and back up your saved KeyLab data

***

<span style="color:#2dc26b"><strong>Switch between Mythic+ and Raid mode to explore results for each type of content.</strong></span>

***

Use `/keylab` or the minimap button to open the journal.
