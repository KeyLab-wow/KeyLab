# KeyLab

## Your gameplay becomes your guide.

Most guides begin with someone else’s character, gear, and results. KeyLab begins with yours.

KeyLab is a personal performance journal that connects planning, practice, real gameplay, and review. It records the setups you actually use and keeps them with the results they produced, allowing you to learn from your own Mythic+ runs, raid pulls, and training-dummy sessions.

It does not prescribe a Best in Slot list or tell you that one build is universally correct. Instead, KeyLab helps you test ideas, follow the results, and decide what you want to try next.

In a way, you become the creator of your own personal guide built from your character, your playstyle, and your actual gameplay.

## The KeyLab Process

### 1\. Choose what you want to try

Start with a talent build, stat priority, gear setup, Macro Sequence, or gearing goal.

Use Gear Planning to review current season gearing information and crafted items. Save specific items in Gear Targets, enter optional secondary stat goals, or use the Stat Goal Matcher to find combinations for your open gear slots.

KeyLab does not determine Best in Slot. The matcher looks for combinations that move the completed gear set toward the secondary stat goals you entered.

### 2\. Test under controlled conditions

Practice Sessions let you record timed tests at a training dummy.

Choose a test length, run your rotation, and save the result with the talent build, character stats, Macro Sequence version, spell queue setting, and recorded combat totals used during that session.

Repeating tests under similar conditions makes it easier to compare changes before taking them into a dungeon or raid.

### 3\. Play the game normally

Complete Mythic+ runs or fight raid bosses as you normally would.

Using Blizzard’s Damage Meter data, KeyLab records the encounter together with the setup and results available at that time. You do not need to maintain a separate spreadsheet or remember which build, stats, or gear you were using.

### 4\. Review what happened

Open Last Run for a summary of your newest Mythic+ run or raid session, including totals, rankings, outcomes, and pull-by-pull information.

Encounters keeps your saved Mythic+ runs and raid boss pulls available for later review.

### 5\. Compare your setups

Talent Builds, Stat Profiles, and Gear Profiles connect your recorded setups to their observed results.

You can compare them using the performance metric that matters to you, including damage, healing, absorbs, interrupts, dispels, avoidable damage, damage taken, or deaths.

These comparisons describe what happened in your saved gameplay. They are not simulations or theoretical rankings.

### 6\. Follow changes over time

Trends compares recent results with earlier saved runs or pulls.

It separates performance, execution, and consistency signals so you can see whether something appears to be improving, remaining steady, or changing and then decide what to test next.

### 7\. Turn the results into your next plan

Use what you learned to adjust your build, practice a different sequence, change your stat goals, or choose new gear targets.

The Gear Dashboard brings your equipped gear, Tier progress, Catalyst options, alternatives, seasonal resources, and saved target progress together in one place.

Then the process begins again:

**Plan → Practice → Play → Review → Compare → Adjust**

## Quality-of-Life Tools

KeyLab also includes several tools designed to reduce the amount of information you need to track outside the game:

*   View official World of Warcraft news, events, tuning notes, and game updates from the Home tab.
*   Search current crafted gear and add selected items to a personal crafting plan.
*   Open your crafted material shopping list automatically while visiting the Auction House.
*   Review required materials, owned resources, and required crests without writing a separate list.
*   See saved gear targets and Tier Catalyst runs while browsing Premade Groups.
*   Build and manage Macro Sequences with groups, modes, versions, and keyboard or mouse button bindings.
*   Minimize the main KeyLab window automatically when other WoW windows open while keeping relevant helper windows available.
*   Keep seasonal records separated and manage saved KeyLab data from Settings.

_The Auction House helper is informational only. KeyLab never searches for, buys, or posts auctions for you._

## Macro Sequencer

The Macro Sequencer lets you create individual macros and arrange them into class- and specialization-based sequences.

You can organize macros into groups, select sequence modes, maintain multiple versions, assign keyboard or mouse-button bindings, and restore recently deleted work from the Recycle Bin.

One press advances one sequence step. World of Warcraft still determines which ability can cast.

## Requirements

KeyLab uses Blizzard’s built-in Damage Meter to save Mythic+ runs, raid boss pulls, and Practice Session results.

Enable both **Damage Meter** and **Auto Reset Damage Meter** under:

**Game Menu → Options → Gameplay Enhancements**

## Open KeyLab

Use **/keylab** or the minimap button to open your journal.
