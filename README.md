# KeyLab

## Your gameplay. Your progress. Your journey.

Follow your favorite guide. Find gear that fits your goals. See what works for you.

KeyLab brings gear planning, guide recommendations, talent switching, practice, and run history together—right inside the game.

Less time keeping notes. More time playing.

## <span style="color:#2DC26B;">Your favorite guides, fewer steps</span>

Browse **Wowhead and Icy Veins gear and talent recommendations** for your class and specialization.

- **Add talent builds** directly to your saved in-game loadouts.
- **Switch builds** before a key starts or between raid pulls, when the game allows.
- **Set Gear Targets** from a guide’s gear list with a button.
- **Configure crafted items** separately and add them to your crafting plan.
- See the original gear recommended for **Catalyst conversion**, not just the finished tier piece.

## <span style="color:#2DC26B;">Follow a gear list—or find your own path</span>

You don’t have to chase one exact list of items.

Use the **Stat Goal Matcher** to find gear combinations that work toward your preferred stat percentages.

- Keep the gear you want and find pieces for the slots you want to replace.
- Build a full gear plan or revisit it as you collect new items.
- Save your main choices as **Targets** and backup choices as **Alternatives**.

Your **Gear Dashboard** brings the plan together: equipped gear, targets, crafted plans, item sources, loot-spec guidance, tier pieces, upgrade tracks, and currencies.

## <span style="color:#2DC26B;">Helpful reminders when you need them</span>

Your saved plans follow you into the game.

- **After a raid boss kill:** see still-needed Targets and Alternatives from that boss to help with loot decisions.
- **After a dungeon:** review your saved items and Nebulous Voidcore balance before leaving.
- **At the Great Vault:** bring up your target reminder while reviewing your options.
- **At the Auction House:** open your crafted-gear shopping list without digging through notes.

## <span style="color:#2DC26B;">Get your group ready</span>

The **Group Dashboard** keeps useful group information together.

- Check player roles, specs, item levels, and visible buffs.
- Review group capabilities, with inherent abilities separated from talent- and pet-dependent options.
- Use the movable **Group Snapshot** for a compact view and quick talent switching.
- Temporarily point a saved support macro at a group member—such as Misdirection on the tank. It returns to its original target when you leave the group.

## <span style="color:#2DC26B;">See what works for you</span>

Stop trying to remember which setup you used three runs ago.

- Review **Last Run, Last Raid, and saved encounters**.
- Look back at the talents, stats, gear, and results from your runs.
- Compare saved builds, profiles, and performance trends.
- Record timed **Practice Sessions** and link the macro sequence you tested.
- Create and organize your own sequences with the **Macro Sequencer**.

Start with a guide’s recommendation, try your own changes, and keep a record of your progress.

## <span style="color:#2DC26B;">Easy to explore</span>

- An optional **guided tour** shows you where to click.
- News, events, game updates, and seasonal information stay close at hand.
- Seasonal record management keeps your gameplay journal organized.

## <span style="color:#2DC26B;">Combat recording setup</span>

KeyLab uses Blizzard’s built-in Damage Meter.

- 
**Game Menu → Options → Gameplay Enhancements**

Open KeyLab with **/keylab** or the minimap button.
