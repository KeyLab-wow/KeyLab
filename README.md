# KeyLab

KeyLab is a one-of-a-kind Mythic+ and Raid performance lab that brings encounter tracking, stat, talent, and gear comparisons, practice testing, gear planning, and press-by-press action sequencing together in one addon.

Compare the setups you actually played, review your results, plan your next gear targets, and keep everything connected to your own character and gameplay.

**Try a build.**  
**Adjust your stats.**  
**Run content.**  
**Review the results.**

Use `/keylab` or the minimap button to open the journal.

---

## Analyze Your Gameplay

- Review saved Mythic+ runs and individual raid boss pulls
- Explore Mythic+ and Raid summaries
- Compare Talent Builds, Stat Profiles, and complete Gear Profiles
- Follow boss-specific and Mythic+ performance Trends
- Run controlled Practice Sessions at training dummies
- Record the sequence and active version used during each test

---

## Plan Your Gear

Browse eligible dungeon and raid loot, save Targets and Alternatives, track Tier progress, and review item sources through the Gear Dashboard.

The advanced **Stat Goal Matcher** evaluates your equipped gear together with your open equipment slots to find the whole-set combination that most closely approaches your chosen secondary-stat goals.

It includes:

- Character-aware, slot-aware gear matching
- Projected character-panel percentages
- Balanced and Favor Priority strategies
- Upgrade-track normalization
- Myth-track preference without allowing incorrect stats to dominate
- Diminishing-return warnings
- Explanations for unreachable goals
- Guidance explaining why the suggested combination is the closest available match

A **Goal Match** is not a Best-in-Slot claim. KeyLab matches eligible dungeon and raid gear to your secondary-stat goals; it does not evaluate Tier bonuses, trinket effects, embellishments, set effects, or every factor considered by a full simulation.

---

## Build Your Own Action Sequences

The **KeyLab Action Sequencer** lets you create class- and specialization-specific sequences from exact multiline World of Warcraft macro blocks.

- One physical press attempts one predetermined sequence step
- World of Warcraft always decides whether the action executes
- Create multiple named versions for different content
- Assign keyboard or mouse-button bindings
- Review all assigned bindings in one place
- Restore deleted sequences and versions from the Recycle Bin
- Connect active sequence versions with Practice Session results

KeyLab does not automate inputs, select abilities, manage the Global Cooldown, or make combat decisions.

---

KeyLab uses information available through World of Warcraft and the Blizzard Damage Meter. It does not require combat-log uploads or an external website.

KeyLab is not a simulation tool or universal Best-in-Slot authority. It is a personal performance, testing, and gearing journal built around your character, your setups, and your real gameplay.
